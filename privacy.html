Privacy policy

This app is a personal configuration of rclone used only by its owner.

  • It accesses only the owner's own Google Drive, with the owner's permission.
  • Files are copied to the owner's own computer. No data is stored on any other server.
  • No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to anyone.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
